Agronomic Standards

Standard Soil Fertility Rating Scale (ICAR)

Recognized Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) benchmarks used on national Soil Health Cards to interpret laboratory test reports.

⚗️ Soil Reaction (pH) Unitless

Acidity & Alkalinity

Logarithmic Hydrogen Ion Activity

📊 Classification Benchmarks:
  • • < 5.5: Strongly Acidic (Lime needed)
  • • 5.5 – 6.5: Moderately Acidic
  • • 6.5 – 7.5: Neutral / Optimum (Peak availability)
  • • 7.5 – 8.5: Moderately Alkaline
  • • > 8.5: Strongly Alkaline / Sodic (Gypsum needed)
Impact: pH determines chemical solubility and root absorption of all macro- and micro-nutrients.
🌱 Primary Macro: N Alkaline Permanganate Method

Available Nitrogen (N)

Readily Mineralizable Organic Nitrogen

📊 Classification Benchmarks:
  • • Low: < 140 kg/ha (Requires split fertilizer)
  • • Medium / Adequate: 140 – 280 kg/ha
  • • High: > 280 kg/ha (Reduce synthetic N)
Function: Fuels vegetative growth, chlorophyll synthesis, and rapid leafy canopy development.
🌾 Primary Macro: P Olsen's P / Bray's Method

Available Phosphorus (P)

Available Phosphate (P₂O₅)

📊 Classification Benchmarks:
  • • Low: < 23 kg/ha P₂O₅ (Basal placement)
  • • Medium / Adequate: 23 – 56 kg/ha P₂O₅
  • • High: > 56 kg/ha P₂O₅ (Skip/reduce P)
Function: Drives early root elongation, energy transfer (ATP), and early reproductive flowering.
🪵 Primary Macro: K Ammonium Acetate Extractable

Available Potassium (K)

Available Potash (K₂O)

📊 Classification Benchmarks:
  • • Low: < 140 kg/ha K₂O (Apply MOP/potash)
  • • Medium / Adequate: 140 – 330 kg/ha K₂O
  • • High: > 330 kg/ha K₂O (Rich reserves)
Function: Regulates cell turgor, drought hardiness, pest resistance, and grain/tuber quality.
